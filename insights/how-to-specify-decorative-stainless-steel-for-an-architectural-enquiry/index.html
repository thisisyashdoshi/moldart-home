How to Specify Decorative Stainless Steel for an Architectural Enquiry

Decorative stainless enquiries become easier to evaluate when the visual target, geometry and service requirement are defined before quotation. The exact finish, coating system and acceptance criteria remain project-specific.

Important: this is a requirement-capture framework, not a universal MOLDART specification for PVD hardness, colour tolerance, coating thickness, adhesion, corrosion performance, AFP performance or fabrication tolerance.

Use this checklist to prepare a clearer enquiry for architectural, interior or fabricated decorative-stainless applications. Where a sample, drawing or measurable acceptance method already exists, include it with the enquiry.

01

Define the product form and nominal dimensions

State whether the requirement is sheet, panel, profile or fabricated component, together with nominal dimensions and thickness. Fabrication tolerances should come from the approved drawing and confirmed process capability rather than a universal default.

02

Define the required surface finish

State the intended surface family such as No.4, Hairline or Mirror, or provide an approved visual/sample reference. Where a decorative PVD or AFP system is required, identify the exact finish or approved sample route rather than relying on a generic colour name alone.

03

Define the coating or treatment basis only where applicable

For PVD/AFP requirements, identify the exact vendor/process system where known. Coating chemistry, colour, thickness, hardness, adhesion, corrosion behaviour and AFP performance are not universal across decorative stainless products and should not be assumed from a generic finish description.

04

Define appearance acceptance

Use an approved physical sample and/or an explicitly specified colour-measurement method and tolerance where measurable colour control is required. This guide does not imply a universal colour-difference threshold.

05

Define fabrication and drawing requirements

Share the approved drawing, edge/bend/profile geometry, visible-face orientation, holes/slots and other fabrication details where applicable. Do not assume a universal bending or fabrication tolerance.

06

Define service environment and application

State the intended architectural/interior/exterior application and any relevant exposure, cleaning or maintenance requirement so that the exact surface/coating route can be technically reviewed.

07

Share quantity, destination and inspection basis

Include quantity, delivery destination, packing expectations and any required inspection/acceptance method before final commercial evaluation.

Prepare the enquiry for technical review

Share the product form, nominal dimensions/thickness, finish or approved sample reference, drawing where applicable, quantity, intended application and delivery destination.

FAQ

Common specification questions

Is one finish name enough to quote decorative stainless steel?

Usually not. A complete enquiry should also define product form, dimensions/thickness, a finish or approved sample reference, drawing requirements where relevant, quantity, application and inspection basis.

Can a generic PVD colour name define the full coating specification?

No. PVD chemistry, colour, thickness, hardness, adhesion and corrosion behaviour depend on the exact qualified system and should be confirmed for the actual product and order.

Does this guide set universal fabrication tolerances?

No. Fabrication tolerances should come from the approved drawing and confirmed process capability for the relevant product and operation.
