Mistakes to avoid

  • Comparing prices before the application and approval reference are fixed.
  • Requesting documents that do not apply to the material or destination.
  • Treating sample approval as a photo instead of a retained reference.
  • Leaving packing and receiving evidence until after dispatch pressure starts.
  • Changing finish, grade, route, or quantity after quote comparison begins.
