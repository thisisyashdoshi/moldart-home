Our Story

For over 35 years, Moldart has set the benchmark for quality and technical precision across two distinct industrial ecosystems — wood and steel.

Built on the principle of integrating international quality with the efficiency of global supply chains and the cost advantage of Indian operations, we deliver a unique value proposition to manufacturers, architects, and industrialists.
