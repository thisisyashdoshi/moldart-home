Steel source notes list SS 201, 304, 316, and 430, with No.4, Hairline, Mirror, PVD, and AFP as decision points.

UseWhere this product actually belongs.
  • Elevator cabins, wall cladding, retail displays, and interior feature surfaces.
  • Projects where grade, finish, environment, and sample approval must move together.
  • Decorative steel work where visual consistency matters across panels.
Quote driversWhat changes comparison and pricing.
  • Grade, finish, and visual approval route are confirmed per project.
  • Keeps grade and finish decisions tied to use condition.
  • Avoids approving a finish image without corrosion or touch-level context.
SpecsFields to confirm before quote.
  • Grades: SS 201 / SS 304, SS 316 on request.
  • Finishes: No.4, Hairline, Mirror.
  • PVD colours: Gold, Rose Gold, Black.
ChecksWhat should be reviewed before approval.
  • Surface direction, scratches, gloss/reflectivity, and colour match.
  • Coating or AFP requirement where applicable.
  • Flatness, edge condition, film protection, and packing.
DocumentsEvidence to request or attach.
  • Latest catalogue or technical sheet version.
  • Sample, finish, drawing, or approved reference.
  • Commercial and handover documents after order release.

Mistakes to avoid

These usually create weak quotations or repeat clarification.

Approving finish before environment and grade.
Using SS 201 vs 304 as a price-only question.
Not defining film protection or site handling.

RFQ completeness

Send open points as assumptions instead of turning them into claims.
