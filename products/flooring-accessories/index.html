Room transitions

Wall skirting • Staircases

What it does
Flooring accessories are the finishing components installed alongside laminate flooring

Flooring accessories are the finishing components installed alongside laminate flooring. They cover expansion gaps, transitions between rooms, wall-to-floor junctions, and staircase edges.

Key checks
Room transitions, expansion gaps, wall junctions, stair details, and installation method required before selection

Profile type, base material, dimensions, finish, and wear surface confirmed in approved technical evidence • Décor coordination and accessory compatibility confirmed against the approved flooring sample

Commercial scope
Reference sheet only

Accessory mix and finish matching are confirmed per programme. Final grade, finish, quantity, and commercial route are confirmed directly.
