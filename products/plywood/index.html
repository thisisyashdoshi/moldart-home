Evidence and release controls

APPROVE BEFORE SCALE-UP.

The RFQ, sample, test method, receiving check and packing basis must describe the same product construction.

Evidence

Evidence and test methods

  • Current TDS or controlled specification with revision and product scope
  • Test or inspection evidence tied to the stated method and product or lot
  • Approved physical sample, drawing or master reference where appearance or fit matters
  • Packing, label and traceability record appropriate to the order
Approval

Sample and trial route

  • Confirm the RFQ inputs and evidence list
  • Review the applicable sample, drawing or technical submission
  • Run the required process or fit trial before scale-up
  • Record the accepted reference, revision and hold criteria
QC

Receiving and hold criteria

  • Verify product identity, construction and ordered variant
  • Check dimensions, visible condition and agreed acceptance points
  • Reconcile documents, labels and batch or lot references
  • Hold material when identity, damage, evidence or sample match is unresolved
Packing

Storage, handling and packing

  • Agree moisture, surface and edge protection before dispatch
  • State roll, sheet, panel, plate or tooling handling limits
  • Keep labels and traceability readable without exposing private counterparties
  • Inspect packing condition at receipt before product release
