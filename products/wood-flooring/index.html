What it does
Engineered wood flooring consists of a decorative surface layer bonded to an HDF core with integrated click-lock profiles

Engineered wood flooring consists of a decorative surface layer bonded to an HDF core with integrated click-lock profiles. It is installed as a floating floor system over prepared subfloors in residential and commercial spaces.
