OVERVIEW

Moldart supplies silicone-copper composite press pads engineered for uniform heat transfer and reliable pressure distribution across the full press area. Designed for high-volume lamination environments, these pads support consistent output quality over extended production cycles.

WHERE IT FITS

Press pads sit between the heating platen and the press plate in lamination presses. They ensure even heat and pressure distribution, which directly affects surface quality and lamination bond strength.

KEY SPECIFICATIONS

  • Silicone-copper composite
  • Approx. 80,000-100,000 cycles
  • Maximum width: 3300 mm
  • Supports uniform heat and pressure

TYPICAL APPLICATIONS

  • • Short-cycle lamination
  • • High-volume pressing
  • • Decorative board manufacturing

CUSTOMIZATION

Widths and construction can be aligned to the press line.

COMMERCIAL NOTES

Rated for approximately 80,000–100,000 pressing cycles. Maximum width of 3300 mm. Widths and construction can be aligned to specific press line requirements.
