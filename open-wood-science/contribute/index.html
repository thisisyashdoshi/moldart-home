Contribution boundary

No confidential factory data, supplier trade secrets, price-sensitive details, or unauthorized paywalled text. Every substantive correction needs a reason plus source.

Private copy only

If approved, you receive one assigned private review copy with tracked changes/comments. Master drafts and parent folders are not shared.

Source-backed edits

Major corrections, numbers, standards, process limits and claims need a reason plus URL, DOI, test method, page/table reference, SDS/COA or legally shareable document.

Public-safe output

Moldart reviews, accepts, rejects or holds every contribution before any final public DOCX/PDF is released.

Review process

  1. Apply with identity, expertise, evidence and conflict details.
  2. Moldart screens the application before any draft access.
  3. Approved reviewers receive one private Word/SharePoint review copy.
  4. Review the full assigned copy with source-backed comments and tracked changes.
  5. Moldart accepts, rejects or holds edits and records the decision.
  6. Only approved, public-safe contributions enter final public documents.

Who should apply

Researchers, factory process experts, resin/formulation specialists, QC/testing labs, standards/compliance experts, supplier technical-service specialists and independent consultants with verifiable experience.

Applications with placeholder profiles, weak evidence, unclear conflicts or confidential material are held or rejected before access.
