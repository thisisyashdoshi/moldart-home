MOLDART | PRESS PLATE INCOMING INSPECTION

Before dispatch / use, define:
- identity and drawing revision
- L × W × t
- flatness / parallelism method where required

- surface condition and protection
- packing / handling evidence
- nonconformance route

Actual methods, locations and acceptance limits remain order- and drawing-specific.
