Supplier Document Pack
Moldart buyer checklist

Applies to: Material and import document checks

Use this checklist to prepare a cleaner RFQ, approval pack, receiving check, or dispatch review. Confirm exact values from supplier test report, approved sample, or buyer project requirement.

Checklist
- TDS when technical specification is needed
- COA when batch chemical/quality data applies
- MTC for metal route proof
- Test report only for agreed performance point
- Packing list and HS discussion for import review
- Product, packing, and loading photos where receiving needs evidence

RFQ CTA
Share product, application, size, finish/design, quantity, destination, incoterm, and document/sample requirements for review.
