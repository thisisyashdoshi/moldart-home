Shuttering Plywood Receiving Checklist
Moldart buyer checklist

Applies to: Formwork and shuttering plywood

Use this checklist to prepare a cleaner RFQ, approval pack, receiving check, or dispatch review. Confirm exact values from supplier test report, approved sample, or buyer project requirement.

Checklist
- Match thickness, face, and edge seal
- Inspect film/overlay and corners
- Record pack condition and board count
- Check storage and handling before use
- Track stripping, cleaning, oiling, repair
- Calculate cost per accepted use

RFQ CTA
Share product, application, size, finish/design, quantity, destination, incoterm, and document/sample requirements for review.
